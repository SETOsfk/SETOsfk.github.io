How much attention does a new album buy — and for how long?

On release day, views of an artist's Wikipedia article jump to a median 4.7× their usual level, against 1.3× on an ordinary Friday. Half of the jump is gone in 5 days. Over eight weeks a typical album adds about 28 days' worth of normal attention. 144 albums by 38 artists; press play to step through them.

Why here and in the headline? The headline is the median of each album's own highest day; albums peak on different days, so the day-by-day median curve is flatter. Usual level = median daily views on days −35 to −8. The band on the stage is the middle half of all albums.

What I found

  1. An album lifts the artist's article to a median 4.7× its usual daily views within two weeks (middle half 2.7–7.5×). Ordinary Fridays: 1.3×.
  2. The jump is short: half gone after 5 days on the median curve, still 1.2× at day 28.
  3. Bigger artists get bigger relative jumps, not smaller ones (rank correlation 0.31 between usual views and peak).
  4. An artist's past albums do not help predict the next one: the median of all earlier albums is typically off by 2.7×, the artist's own history by 3.4× (103 out-of-sample forecasts).

Read with care

  • Wikipedia views measure attention, not listening, sales or chart position. A news story moves them as much as a record.
  • English Wikipedia only, so attention from non-English readers (e.g. Bad Bunny, Diljit Dosanjh) is undercounted.
  • 51 well-known artists, mostly US/UK; no Turkish artists, no newcomers. Results do not generalise to small artists.
  • Album dates are MusicBrainz's earliest release date; a few are a day or two off the official date (e.g. a surprise drop or an early regional release).
  • This is a before/after comparison, not proof that the album caused every view: promotion, singles and tours cluster around releases.
  • When singles or a tour lift the four weeks before release, the 'usual level' is too high and the album looks smaller: 10 albums end up with negative extra attention (e.g. 1989 (Taylor's Version), released during the Eras Tour).
Method

Event study, 144 albums. Baseline = median daily views on days −35..−8; peak = highest day 0..13 ÷ baseline; extra attention = Σ(views − baseline) over days 0..55 ÷ baseline. Comparison: 1,538 ordinary Fridays, same metrics. Forecast check: rolling origin, simplest methods first. Full write-up in BULGULAR.md.
